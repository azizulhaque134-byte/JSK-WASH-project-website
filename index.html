Our WASH Services

Sustainable Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Services

Water Supply

Ensuring access to safe drinking water through deep tube wells, piped water supply systems, regular water quality monitoring, and the daily operation, repair, and preventive maintenance of 11 deep tube wells to provide a continuous and sustainable water service.

Sanitation

Ensuring safe and dignified sanitation services through the construction, rehabilitation, and routine maintenance of sanitation facilities, including the daily repair and maintenance of 106 latrines and 74 bathing cubicles to maintain functionality, cleanliness, and accessibility.

Hygiene Promotion

Strengthening community health through hygiene promotion activities, including Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM), handwashing campaigns, household waste segregation awareness, and education on the five critical times for handwashing to encourage sustainable behaviour change and disease prevention.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Building Healthier Communities Through WASH

The WASH Project improves access to safe drinking water, sanitation facilities, hygiene promotion, environmental health, and community resilience through sustainable infrastructure and community participation.

Water

Safe drinking water supply and water quality monitoring.

Sanitation

Hygienic and climate-resilient sanitation facilities.

Hygiene

Awareness sessions, MHM and handwashing promotion.

Environment

Waste management and clean community initiatives.

Our Mission

To ensure equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene services for vulnerable communities through sustainable and inclusive interventions.

Our Vision

Healthy communities where everyone enjoys safe water, improved sanitation, dignity and a better quality of life.
